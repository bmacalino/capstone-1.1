# Design — DeFaketive

## Direction

**Forensic Ledger** is a ground-up identity for DeFaketive. It treats every screen as a working evidence file: warm archival paper, ink-black structure, ultraviolet signals, hard rules, oversized verdict language, and compact monospaced annotations. It should feel investigative and editorial—not like a generic analytics dashboard.

## Macrostructure

- Public site: an editorial front page with a persistent black index rail, an oversized thesis, evidence intake, and a numbered research sequence.
- Product: a dense workbench with a fixed case rail and task-first surfaces.
- Reports: an evidence document with a strong verdict, provenance, excerpts, and methodology arranged for reading rather than decoration.

## Visual contract

- Warm paper is the default field; black is structural, not merely text.
- Ultraviolet marks actions, active states, and focus. It never implies risk.
- Green, amber, and red are reserved for evidence meaning.
- Display: Space Grotesk 600–700. Body: IBM Plex Sans 400–600. Metadata: JetBrains Mono 500–600.
- Square corners and hard rules are the default. Shadows are offset and graphic, never soft elevation.
- Hierarchy comes from decisive scale changes, not stacks of similar headings.

## Motion and interaction

- Motion is limited to scan progress, navigation reveal, and direct state transitions.
- Reduced-motion users receive immediate state changes.
- Focus is always visible. Progress is factual and success is quiet.

## Shared contract

Every view shares the eye mark, paper/ink/ultraviolet palette, typography roles, hard-rule construction, evidence-first hierarchy, and precise decision-support language. Marketing may be expressive; the workbench and reports stay dense and functional.

## Export

The canonical vanilla-CSS export is `tokens.css`; `static/css/tokens.css` is its runtime mirror.
