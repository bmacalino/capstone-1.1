Four stages keep the score connected to the reviews behind it.

02

Every review is classified and checked against a Taglish defect dictionary.

Shop smarter

A

Defect clusters

Repeated “overheating,” “not charging,” and “sira” reports across reviews.

B

Counterfeit slang

Taglish warning terms such as “peke” and “scam” that generic models miss.

C

Sentiment spikes

A rising share of negative experiences within the collected review sample.

D

Rating mismatch

High star ratings paired with complaint-heavy review text.

QUESTIONS

Before you rely on a report

Is DeFaketive really free to use?+

Yes. Guests get three scans per day, while a free account unlocks unlimited analyses.

How accurate is the Risk Score?+

It summarizes sentiment and product-risk signals in the collected review sample. Use it as decision support, not proof.

What's the difference between guest and signed-in?+

The workspace brings together product analysis, saved reports, and your wishlist.

Which platforms are supported?+

DeFaketive supports product links from Shopee PH, Lazada PH, and Temu.

What happens if a product scores high-risk?+

Review the highlighted evidence and compare lower-risk alternatives before deciding to buy.
